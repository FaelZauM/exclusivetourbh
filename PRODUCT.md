# ExclusiveTour BH

## Register

brand

## Users

Professionals and travelers needing premium executive transportation in Belo Horizonte — airport transfers, corporate travel, city tours, and intercity trips. They value punctuality, comfort, safety, and local expertise when choosing a transportation service. Primary context: mobile or desktop, researching or booking on demand.

## Product Purpose

A premium one-page landing site for ExclusiveTour BH that converts visitors into WhatsApp leads. Every section builds trust through visual quality, local credentials (Faixa do Move accreditation, 90+ vehicles), and service transparency — funneling toward one action: contact via WhatsApp.

## Brand Personality

Executive, Confident, Exclusive. The brand speaks with the quiet authority of a service that doesn't need to shout. Warmth comes through service quality, not design gimmicks.

## Anti-references

No generic, templated, or robotic feel. Avoid the look of AI-generated, corporate cookie-cutter transportation sites. The design must feel bespoke, curated, and human — like the service itself.

## Design Principles

1. **Arrive in style** — every visual element reinforces the premium experience. Typography, spacing, and imagery should feel as refined as the ride.
2. **Trust through transparency** — credentials (Faixa do Move, 90+ fleet, 5★ ratings) are front and center, not buried.
3. **One tap away** — WhatsApp is the only conversion; every section leads naturally toward contact, without hard-selling.
4. **Local soul, global polish** — Belo Horizonte roots with world-class execution. The site should feel unmistakably BH while competing with any international luxury brand.
5. **Fast by default** — performance is a feature. This is a lightweight, one-page site that loads instantly and scrolls buttery smooth.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance. Adequate color contrast (≥4.5:1 body text), semantic HTML, keyboard-navigable, reduced motion support. Portuguese language as primary.
